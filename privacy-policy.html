Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Equinox Tech s. r. o. ("Equinox Tech", "we", "us") processes personal data in connection with the website equinox.tech (the "Website"). We are committed to processing personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the "GDPR") and applicable Slovak law.

1. Data controller

Equinox Tech s. r. o., with its registered seat at Jarabinková 6B, 821 09 Bratislava – Ružinov, Slovak Republic, IČO 57 762 554, entered in the Commercial Register of the City Court Bratislava III, Section Sro, Insert No. 201674/B, is the controller of personal data processed through the Website.

You can contact the controller in writing at the registered seat above for any matter relating to this Policy or to the exercise of your rights.

2. What data we process

The Website is a static informational site. It does not host contact forms, user accounts, comment sections, advertising networks or third-party analytics or tracking scripts, and it does not set cookies.

As with any website, our hosting provider automatically records limited technical information in server log files when the Website is accessed. This may include:

  • the IP address of the requesting device;
  • the date and time of the request;
  • the page or file requested and the HTTP status returned;
  • the referring page, where transmitted by your browser;
  • the browser type, version and operating system (user-agent string).

If you contact us directly (for example, by postal correspondence sent to our registered seat), we process the contact details and the content of your message solely to handle your enquiry.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Operating and securing the Website — delivering page content, maintaining stability and detecting or preventing abuse and attacks. Legal basis: our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in providing a secure and functional website.
  • Responding to enquiries — communicating with you about a request you have sent us. Legal basis: our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, or steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Compliance — meeting legal obligations to which we are subject. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

4. Recipients and processors

Personal data contained in server logs is processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers acting as processors on our instructions under Article 28 GDPR. We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may disclose data where required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

5. International transfers

Where a processor is located outside the European Economic Area, any transfer is made subject to appropriate safeguards under Chapter V GDPR, such as an adequacy decision of the European Commission or Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

Server log data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes above, typically for a short period, and is then deleted or aggregated into non-identifying statistics. Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle the matter and to comply with applicable retention obligations.

7. Your rights

Subject to the conditions set out in the GDPR, you have the right to request access to your personal data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, contact us in writing at our registered seat. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky), Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. The current version is always published on this page with the "last updated" date shown above.
