How we work

A balanced approach — the equinox principle

An equinox is the point where day and night hold equal weight. We apply the same idea to delivery: speed balanced with rigour, ambition with maintainability.

Engineering-first

Clean architecture, tested code and documentation are the default, not an upsell. What we hand over is meant to be owned and extended by your team.

Transparent delivery

Short iterations, visible progress and honest estimates. You always know what is done, what is next and what it costs.

EU-based & compliant

Registered in the Slovak Republic and operating under EU law, with GDPR-aware data handling built into every engagement.

Remote-first, senior-led

Small, senior teams working asynchronously across time zones — the same people from first call to production.

Right-sized solutions

We match the technology to the problem. No over-engineering, and no shortcuts that turn into tomorrow's debt — the balance point between them.

Secure by default

Least-privilege access, dependency hygiene and hardened delivery pipelines are part of every engagement, not a separate phase bolted on at the end.

The company

About Equinox Tech

Equinox Tech s. r. o. is an independent information-technology company headquartered in Bratislava, Slovakia. It was established in 2026 as a private limited liability company and is entered in the Commercial Register maintained by the City Court Bratislava III.

The company is owned and led by its two founders and provides computer services and data processing, technology and business consulting, digital marketing and market research, trade and service intermediation, and organizational-economic support — the full scope recorded in its registration.

Equinox Tech works with clients in Slovakia, across the European Union and internationally, on a remote-first basis.
