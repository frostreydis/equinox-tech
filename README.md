# Equinox Tech s. r. o.

**Equinox Tech** is an independent information-technology company based in Bratislava,
Slovakia. We design, build and operate custom software, data pipelines and cloud
infrastructure, and advise the teams that run them.

The company name comes from the *equinox* — the point at which day and night hold equal
weight. It reflects how we approach delivery: speed balanced with rigour, ambition with
maintainability.

## What we do

Equinox Tech provides IT services across the full delivery lifecycle. Each service line
corresponds to the company's registered scope of business:

- **Custom software development** — web applications, APIs and internal platforms, from
  architecture and prototyping through to production and long-term support.
- **Data engineering & automation** — computer services related to data processing: ETL
  pipelines, integrations, reporting layers and workflow automation.
- **Cloud & DevOps** — infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD, observability and cost control.
- **Technology & management consulting** — activities of business, organizational and
  economic advisors: technical due diligence, architecture reviews and team enablement.
- **Digital marketing & research** — advertising, marketing and information services with
  market and public-opinion research, including analytics instrumentation.
- **Sourcing & operations support** — intermediary activity in trade and services, plus
  administrative and organizational-economic back-office for IT.

## A balanced approach — the equinox principle

An equinox is the point where day and night hold equal weight. We apply the same idea to
delivery: speed balanced with rigour, ambition with maintainability.

- **Engineering-first** — clean architecture, tested code and documentation are the
  default, not an upsell. What we hand over is meant to be owned and extended by your team.
- **Transparent delivery** — short iterations, visible progress and honest estimates. You
  always know what is done, what is next and what it costs.
- **EU-based & compliant** — registered in the Slovak Republic and operating under EU law,
  with GDPR-aware data handling built into every engagement.
- **Remote-first, senior-led** — small, senior teams working asynchronously across time
  zones — the same people from first call to production.
- **Right-sized solutions** — we match the technology to the problem. No over-engineering,
  and no shortcuts that turn into tomorrow's debt — the balance point between them.
- **Secure by default** — least-privilege access, dependency hygiene and hardened delivery
  pipelines are part of every engagement, not a separate phase bolted on at the end.

Equinox Tech works with clients in Slovakia, across the European Union and internationally.
